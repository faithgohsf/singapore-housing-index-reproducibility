# Executive-flat definition verification and decision

18 October 2026. Verification and frozen-input comparison preceded the primary correction. The 2021 article assigns public-housing types above five rooms to room-equivalent 6. HDB has distinct 5room_sold, exec_sold and multigen_sold fields. The freshly downloaded public notebook lists these fields but contains no SHI arithmetic; it supports neither executable weight. The published definition and distinct source categories support correcting only exec_sold to 6. Ordinary five-room flats remain 5 and multi-generation remains 6. The source does not separate executive apartments from maisonettes; both receive the executive mapping. The article's mention of executive condominiums does not authorise recoding private or unmatched housing in this HDB-only study. No contradictory source-category evidence was found.

The score increment is exactly exec_sold / total_dwelling_units. Definition, not fit or agreement, determines the correction. The legacy-versus-recommended comparison now holds executive weight 6 constant while testing studio/other-rental changes. The alternative executive-weight-5 sensitivity is separate.
